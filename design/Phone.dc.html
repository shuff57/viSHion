Q3

The 95% confidence interval for the mean commute time is (18.4, 24.1) minutes. This means we are 95% confident that the true mean commute for all students at the school is between 18.4 and 24.1 minutes.

It does not mean that 95% of students commute in that range. If we took many random samples and built an interval from each one, about 95% of those intervals would capture the true mean.
