# Accessibility

egui on the web draws to a canvas, so the page has no accessibility tree of its own. Every frame the screen
describes itself as `twin::Node`s (plan rule 3) and `web.rs` reconciles them into a hidden DOM, `#controls`.
The canvas is `aria-hidden` and `tabindex=-1`; the twin is what keyboards and assistive technology use.

The same nodes feed the native window (see "Native window" below), so the Windows build has a tree of its own.

## What the twin exposes

| Part | Twin |
|---|---|
| Landmarks (`a11y::landmark`) | `banner` (header), `main` holding `region "Page image"` and `region "Transcript"`, `complementary "Word panel"` (tray), `dialog` + `aria-modal` while a dialog is open. Behind a dialog the banner, main and tray are `inert` and `aria-hidden`: no landmark, heading or Tab stop of the screen is reachable until it closes |
| Headings | one `h1` (the student, or the screen), `h2` for the page and the word panel, `h3` for empty states |
| Groups and lists (`a11y::group_of`) | `group "Layout"`, `"Which text to show"`, `"Zoom"`, `"Which pages"`, `"Theme"`; `list "Pages"`, `"Choices for this word"`, `"Students"` with `<li>` per item |
| Roles per control (`Reg::role`) | `button`; `textbox`; `checkbox` / `switch` (`aria-checked`); `listitem` (a button inside a list item). Toggles are buttons with `aria-pressed` |
| Transcript | one `<p>` per line; flagged words are buttons inline in their sentence; struck-through text (`~~probably~~`) is a `<del>`; math is `role=math` with `aria-label` from `a11y::tex_speech` and the TeX as its text. Spoken math keeps scope: "the sum from i equals 1 to n of x sub i", "x to the power of a plus b end of power plus c", "absolute value of x minus 3 end absolute value", "the fraction the fraction 1 over 2 end fraction over 3 end fraction" (an inner fraction is always closed; a short "1 over 2" only when nothing can attach to it, so a half followed by x is "the fraction 1 over 2 end fraction x"), "the 2 by 2 matrix, row 1: a, b; row 2: c, d; end matrix" (also determinants and cases), "n choose k" |
| Page | the page image is `role=img` "The scanned handwritten page" laid over the stage; the zoom button is named "Fit the page to the pane, zoom 100%"; the student's "2 pages, 96 words" line is a paragraph under the h1 |
| Live regions | `#announce` (polite) and `#announce-alert` (assertive) are always in the page; `Ctl::say` / `say_node` feed them: decisions, who the student is now, which page, the toast, "Saved.", a failed save. A decision also says where the panel moved on to ("Used the suggested word. Now on page 2 of 2. Next, word 2 of 4: a word that could not be read. Nothing is chosen for it yet."; the page is named only when it changed), so a second Enter is never blind; moving a page says "Moved to position 2 of 2." The toast is spoken only through the live region (it is not a paragraph of the transcript). Counts (`student-status`, `tray-count`) and the save state are plain paragraphs read in place, never live regions of their own (round 5: one decision used to write three of them). Errors (`*-error`) are `role=alert`. What belongs to one action is one utterance: the sentences of a frame are joined into one write, "Marked as checked." leads the next student's name (`Ctl::say_node_after`), and a second write within 300 ms is appended to the first instead of replacing it (a live region rewritten twice in a moment is usually read as the second write alone) |
| Focus | `focusin`/`focusout` on the twin drive the canvas focus ring; a control that disappears or is disabled by its own action hands focus on (new screen: its `h1`; same group: the control beside it, so "Zoom in" at 400% lands on "Fit the page"; else the neighbour in its region) and says "Zoom in is not available now." Chrome moves `activeElement` to the body only at its next focus fix-up, so the twin does not trust a disabled button that still reads as active; a dialog takes focus on its first control, keeps Tab inside it and returns focus to what opened it; Tab is never trapped by the canvas |
| Text boxes | `typed-word` holds what the canvas box holds. The twin follows when the screen clears the box (a decision used the typed word) even though the box has focus: `data-app-value` is the value the twin was last told, so a box the person is typing in is left alone and one the app changed is rewritten. A screen reader never reads a value that is not on screen |
| Title | `document.title` and the window title are "`<assignment>` \| viSHion" while an assignment is open and "viSHion" otherwise (js/main.js, WCAG 2.4.2) |
| Focus is on screen | the twin is `overflow: clip`, so the browser cannot scroll it: when a control takes focus the canvas scrolls its scroll area to it (`Ctl::flush_scroll`, applied at the end of the scroll area the control sits in), clear of the sticky word panel; a tall region (the transcript) needs its top on screen. The page stage ring is drawn inside its pane, and the transcript ring shows after Tab (`:focus-visible`) and hides after a mouse click |
| Geometry | every node is laid over the canvas at the rectangle it is drawn in, for touch exploration, magnifier tracking and the highlight of a screen reader: controls (`Reg::at/size`, at least 44 x 44) and, since round 6, headings, lines, counts, legend and notes (`twin::geo`, `Ctl::heading/status/text/end_para` take the drawn `Rect`). A paragraph is positioned and its inline controls are placed relative to it (`web::reconcile` carries the origin); landmarks, groups and lists take the union of what they hold (`web::contain`, `tree::bounds`). Natively the same rectangles are the `BoundingRectangle` of Text nodes; the runs of a line share the line's. `#controls` has `pointer-events: none` and `overflow: clip` |
| Preferences | `prefers-reduced-motion` sets egui `animation_time = 0`; `prefers-contrast: more` selects `LIGHT_HC` / `DARK_HC`; `forced-colors` draws the canvas in the user's own system colours (`web::forced_tokens`: `Canvas` for every surface, `CanvasText` for ink and rules, `GrayText`, `LinkText`). Read once at start (`#controls[data-reduced-motion]`, `[data-high-contrast]`) |
| Zoom | the layout is in CSS pixels: 200% is a 640 wide viewport, 400% is 320. The page tools wrap below 354 points rather than run off the edge. In a window under 300 points high (400% on a laptop) the header is the top of the page and scrolls with it, the word panel stays in the page, and the panes stack. `web::fit_scale` keeps an egui point equal to a CSS pixel even when a browser sizes the canvas backing store differently from `devicePixelRatio`; `web::place` scales the twin if they still differ |

Round 4 additions:

- the same words twice are two announcements: every write to the toast bumps `Ctl::toast_serial`, and `App::announce` speaks the toast again when the serial moved (Copy text, Straighten, "Zoom in is not available now." again);
- the wrapped header is measured (`Ctl::header_h`); when it is taller than half the window it moves into the page, as in a short window, instead of being clipped in a panel with Settings drawn under the page pane (320 x 568);
- a dimmed control says why: `a11y::disabled_reason` / `group_description` give `aria-description` on the "Original" button and on its group (and `title`, as the JS group has), `Tree::description` natively;
- native Tab is the screen's (`App::filter_tab` takes Tab out of egui's input, `twin::next_stop` picks the next enabled control or key region in reading order): the transcript before the word panel, no unnamed egui scroll area or splitter stops, wraps at the ends;
- native landmarks and heading levels: accesskit_windows 0.15 exposes only control type, name, focus and patterns (no landmark type, heading level, description), so on Windows the name carries them (`native::platform_name`: "Maya R., heading level 1", "banner landmark", "Word panel, complementary landmark", "Original, unavailable: Nothing has been corrected ..."). Other platforms get role and level natively. `native-a11y.ps1` asserts the names.

The review screen has no undo: choosing another option for a word (re-choosing "written") is the undo, and it is
announced like any decision, with the word the panel lands on.

Round 5 additions:

- Rotate this page ends with `Event::Saved` (backend answers `Many([Image, Saved])`, a failure is `SaveFailed`), so the header leaves "Saving..." and says "Saved.";
- the zoomed page image pans from the keyboard while the stage has focus: arrows by a line, Page Up/Down by most of the view, Home/End to the top and bottom (`Act::Pan`, as `.scan-stage` scrolls in the JS app);
- Change student: a refused Save says "Choose a student, or type a name." (assertive) and puts focus on the list or the name box; "A new student..." moves focus to the name box and says a text box appeared;
- Next and Previous word to check say which word ("Word 2 of 4: spelling to check, populaton. Nothing is chosen for it yet.") and, when the page changed, "Now on page 2 of 2.";
- spoken math: a bar with no partner is "given" (`P(A|B)`), "such that" inside braces and "divides" otherwise; `	o` is "to" except under `\lim`; the common symbol commands (`\emptyset`, `orall`, `\Rightarrow`, floors, `\mid`, `\subseteq`, `\iint`, ...) have words; `1rac{1}{2}` is "1 and 1 over 2";
- the legend ("Spelling to check, Couldn't read, You checked it"), the token and time stat and the hint under the transcript are text in the twin; empty states are `h2` (no `h1` to `h3` jump);
- the Settings dialog has the JS fields: theme, server address, API key, model name, time limit, straighten, demo, how to suggest, course words (comma separated, for the assignment's topic), likely-word and remembered-fix options with "Clear remembered fixes" (asks first), the three export defaults and "Keep untouched originals". Text boxes are saved when the dialog closes; buttons and toggles at once and say what they set. Not yet: the provider list, "Find models", "Test connection" and the folder chooser (they need the net crate and a folder picker in the host, phase 6; until then a screen-reader user sets the server address by typing it).

Round 6 additions:

- native crash, round 7 (blocker): opening any dialog from the keyboard (Enter on Settings, Remove this page, Change student) with a UI Automation client polling `FocusedElement` killed the window with the same assertion. Root cause is not a focus missing from the update (it was in it) but `accesskit_consumer`: when the focused button moved from the banner to the hidden container (the twin shows only the dialog), the old parent's dropped child became an orphan and its subtree was removed even though the new parent of the same update listed it; the order of nodes in an update is a hash map's, so it depended on ids. `crates/vendor/accesskit_consumer` removes what the root no longer reaches (see `vendor/PATCHES.md`). `cargo test -p vishion-ui native_dialog_opened` feeds the Tab, Enter, Escape updates to a consumer for the three dialogs; `native-a11y-critic-dialogs.ps1` is the real client.
- native dialog focus, round 8: arrow keys inside a modal dialog step along the dialog's tab order (`App::filter_tab`; a text box keeps them for the caret), because egui's own arrow navigation reached the unnamed widgets behind the scrim; a dialog opened with the mouse hands focus back to the control that was activated (`Ctl::activated`), since a click does not give a button egui focus. `cargo test -p vishion-ui native_arrow_keys native_dialog_opened_with_the_mouse`; `native-a11y-critic-dialogs-focus.ps1` is the real client. High-contrast themes: done (`native::forced_tokens`).
- native crash (blocker): egui keeps a focus it was given for one frame even when nothing draws that widget (a choice of the word the panel just left), so its AccessKit update named a focus node the tree did not contain and `accesskit_consumer` asserted in the window thread (`tree.rs:35`, exit -1073740791) with a UI Automation client attached. `native::publish` lets go of a focus that is not a node of this frame. `cargo test -p vishion-ui native_focus_on` reproduces it without a client; `native-a11y-critic-choices.ps1 [-Poll]` is the real one (Enter on choices, with the client reading `FocusedElement` between presses);
- native, round 9: Enter in the new-student name box keeps focus in the box (egui's single-line text box surrenders it; the dialog re-requests it), and Escape is taken out of egui's input natively (`filter_tab`, like Tab) because egui answers it by dropping focus to the window; the screen still sees it as a key, so dialogs and the tray close as before, and on a plain control nothing happens, as in JS. `native_enter_in_the_name_box_keeps_focus`, `native_escape_on_a_plain_control_keeps_focus`; `native-a11y-critic-dialogs-keys.ps1` is the real client.
- native tree, UI Automation `FindAll` returned ~4500 nodes with Settings open: the label "Between pages" and the choice "A Page N line" shared the id `st-sep-label`, one node with two parents, and a client that follows children saw the lower sections over and over. The label is `st-sep-title` now (the choice keeps its id); `native_tree_has_one_parent_per_node_and_no_cycles` checks every node has one parent for the screen and the three dialogs. FindAll with Settings open is ~56 nodes.
- focus after a decision is on the word the panel moved to (`word-<page>-<key>`), where Enter only opens it again, never the same place in the next word's list of choices (a second Enter used to pick another kind of answer unheard). JS re-renders the tray and loses focus; this is better and is the one place the twin is stricter than JS;
- text before the app is running: `index.html` has a visible "Loading viSHion..." (`role=status`) that the app removes when it starts, and when it cannot start (no WebGL: remote desktop, blocklisted GPU, locked-down browser) a `role=alert` says why in words, with the technical message under it, instead of a blank page;
- Shift+Enter and Ctrl+Enter on a page thumbnail are Shift+click and Ctrl+click: the DOM click carries its modifiers (the `act:` message gets a control-A and `c` or `s`, `Ctl::dom_mods`), so "Export N selected", "Review again" and "Clear" are reachable from the keyboard;
- Transcribe this page again (and Retry, and a batch run) is spoken: "Reading this page. The text appears as it is read.", "The page was transcribed. 3 words to check.", and, assertive, "This page could not be transcribed. <why>" (`App::announce_runs`, from the open page's status and a hash of its transcript, so a transcript replaced in one step is heard too);
- test hygiene: the typed-decision test used to type as soon as the text box existed, but the box is on the page for every word; typing before the click on the flagged word had been applied went to the word that was open (or was cleared by the pick). The test now waits for the panel's note to be about the word it opened.

## Native window

`eframe` has the `accesskit` feature, but egui only gives AccessKit an unnamed node per widget (the Windows
tree used to be 50 `ControlType.Custom` nodes with no name). `ui::tree::of` turns the same twin nodes into a
platform tree as plain data (banner, `main` with "Page image" and "Transcript", "Word panel", dialog; groups and
lists; headings with their level; paragraphs with flagged words as buttons inline; math named by its spoken
form with the TeX as its value; toggles with their state; the text box with its value), and `ui::native`
writes it into AccessKit each frame, only while a screen reader is attached. Rules that cost a crash to learn:

- a control keeps the egui widget's own node id, so `Action::Default` from Narrator or NVDA is a click on that
  widget, and focus follows egui's;
- every node egui made this frame must stay reachable from the root or AccessKit's consumer panics in the
  window thread: the ones the twin does not use go under a hidden container, and a widget's own child nodes
  (the text runs of a text box) are kept;
- the window title is the assignment (`ViewportCommand::Title`), and announcements are a polite live `Status`
  node whose name changes with each one.

Automated: `cargo test -p vishion-ui native` (names, roles, levels, a press from the tree, reading order, and
every update of a session accepted by `accesskit_consumer`), and on Windows with a release build:

```
cargo build --release -p vishion-app --bin vishion
powershell -File crates/app/native-a11y.ps1   # launches --demo, walks UI Automation, invokes "Zoom in", rectangles of text
powershell -NoProfile -STA -File crates/app/native-a11y-critic.ps1          # Tab order and names
powershell -NoProfile -STA -File crates/app/native-a11y-critic-choices.ps1 -Poll   # repeated Enter in the word panel, FocusedElement polled
```

macOS (VoiceOver) and Linux (Orca) take the same AccessKit tree and are not run here: do the manual pass below
with the native build too.


Not verifiable here: real NVDA / VoiceOver / Narrator speech (the manual pass below) and contrast of text drawn on the canvas (axe cannot see it). Forced colours are checked in headless Chrome with `forcedColors: 'active'`: the canvas is drawn in the system `Canvas` colour (`a11y-critic.mjs`). Native Windows contrast themes are read once at start (`native::forced_tokens`: `SPI_GETHIGHCONTRAST`, then `GetSysColor` Window / WindowText / GrayText / Hotlight into `theme::forced`, the same mapping as the web `forced-colors`); not run in CI (it needs a contrast theme switched on), so check it by hand: Settings > Accessibility > Contrast themes, start `vishion --demo`.

axe-core cannot see the canvas, so contrast of drawn text is not covered by it: the tokens are checked by eye in
`design/` and the forced-colour variant raises the greys to the ink colour.

## Manual screen reader pass (before each release)

Screen readers cannot run headless. Use the demo assignment (`/?demo=1`), Chrome or Edge, 100% zoom, then repeat
the first two items at 200%. Record the reader, version and browser in the release notes.

### NVDA (Windows, latest) with Chrome

1. Start NVDA, open the app. The title "viSHion" is read. Press `D` to cycle landmarks: banner, main, "Page image",
   "Transcript", "Word panel" are reached in that order.
2. Press `H`: "Maya R.", heading level 1, then the level 2 headings. Press `1` jumps to the h1.
3. Browse mode, down arrow through the transcript: each line is read as one paragraph; a flagged word reads as
   "Spelling to check: confidance. Show options., button" inside its sentence; math reads as words ("x bar plus or
   minus 1.96 times the fraction ...").
4. `B` / `F`: jump between buttons and the text box. "Page 1, 3 words to check" is "pressed". "Layout" announces as a
   group. The page strip announces "Pages, list, 2 items".
5. Focus a flagged word and press Enter (focus mode on): the word panel updates; the text box is named "Type
   what the word says". Choose a suggestion: "Used the suggested word." is spoken, then the new count
   ("3 words to check") and focus stays on a control.
   The next word is named in the same breath ("Next, word 2 of 4: a word that could not be read. Nothing is chosen
   for it yet."): press Enter again only after hearing what it is.
6. Close the panel with Escape: focus is back on the word. Open Settings: "Settings, dialog" is read, focus is on
   the first theme button, Tab goes round inside the dialog, the screen behind it cannot be reached with the
   virtual cursor (H, D, arrow keys), Escape returns to the gear button.
7. Press "Done, next student": "Marked as checked." then the new student ("Omar K., student 4 of 4").
8. Open the assignment overview ("Back to ..."): the screen heading is read at once; row checkboxes read
   "Select Maya R., checkbox, not checked".
9. Tab out of the page and back: nothing traps; Shift+Tab from the first control leaves the page.
10. Windows contrast theme on (Aquatic, Night sky) in Chrome or Edge: the canvas takes the theme's background and text colours, the page is readable, focus ring visible, flagged words still stand out (underline, not colour alone).
11. Settings: Tab through every field; the text boxes are named ("Server address", "Model name", "Course words for ..."), toggles read as checkboxes with their state, the export choices as groups with one pressed. Close with "Done" and reopen: what was typed is kept.
12. Zoom the page image to 400%, focus it (Tab to "Page image") and use the arrow keys, Page Down and End: the image moves; focus does not leave the page stage.
13. "Change student": press Save with nothing chosen: "Choose a student, or type a name." is spoken and focus is on the list. Choose "A new student...": a text box is announced and has focus.
14. "Next word to check" across the page break: the word, the kind of problem and "Now on page 2 of 2." are spoken once.
15. "Rotate this page": the header goes from "Saving..." to the saved state and "Saved." is spoken.
16. Reload with the cache cleared (or throttle the network): while the page downloads, "Loading viSHion" is read; it goes away when the app is up. In a browser with WebGL off (`chrome://flags` or a remote desktop) the page reads "viSHion could not start: this browser has no WebGL ..." and nothing else.
17. Focus a page thumbnail and press Shift+Enter, then Shift+Enter on the next page: "Export 2 selected", "Review again" and "Clear" appear in the strip and are reachable with Tab.
18. "Transcribe this page again" on an untouched page: "Reading this page..." then "The page was transcribed. N words to check." are spoken. Pick a choice in the word panel with Enter: focus is on the next word (read as "Spelling to check: ... Show options."), pressing Enter again does not choose anything.
19. Touch (iOS VoiceOver, TalkBack): drag one finger across the screen: the heading "Maya R.", each line of the transcript, the counts and the legend are announced under the finger, where they are drawn, not at the top left.

### VoiceOver (macOS Safari and Chrome; iOS Safari)

1. Rotor (VO+U): Landmarks lists banner, main, "Page image", "Transcript", "Word panel"; Headings lists the h1 and h2s;
   Form Controls lists the text box and buttons.
2. VO+Right through the transcript reads line by line; the flagged word is a button in its sentence.
3. Activate with VO+Space on a flagged word and a choice; hear "Used the suggested word."
4. Dialogs: Settings is announced as a dialog; VO cannot reach the screen behind it (check the Headings and
   Landmarks rotors: no "Maya R." heading, no main); Escape closes it.
5. iOS: explore by touch over the page tools; each is announced where it is drawn (the twin is laid over the
   canvas), targets are at least 44 points. Swipe right through the page in reading order.
6. Zoom the page to 200% and 400%: nothing is cut off, the tools wrap to more rows.

### Narrator and NVDA with the native window (Windows)

Run `target/release/vishion.exe --demo`. The title bar reads "Unit 5 written response | viSHion".

1. Narrator (Win+Ctrl+Enter): Caps+Right through the page. The banner buttons, "Maya R." (heading), each line
   of the transcript and the flagged words are read; Caps+F7 lists headings and links.
2. Landmarks: banner, main, "Page image", "Transcript", "Word panel".
3. Press a flagged word with Caps+Enter or Space: the word panel updates; after a choice "Used the suggested
   word. Now on page 2 of 2. Next, word 1 of 3..." is spoken (the page only when it changed).
4. Tab reaches every button; the focus ring and the reader's cursor agree. "Zoom in" at the largest size hands
   focus to "Fit the page" and says so.
5. NVDA on the same window: object navigation (NVDA+Numpad 4/6) shows named buttons and no "unknown" objects.

### What to file

For any step that fails: reader and browser versions, the step number, what was said, what was expected, and the
output of `await page.locator('#controls').ariaSnapshot()` for the same state if it is reproducible in Chrome.
